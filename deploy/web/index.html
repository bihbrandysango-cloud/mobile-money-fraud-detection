🛡️ Is this mobile money transaction safe?

Thieves who get into a mobile money account usually send the money to a new account and cash it out fast. Answer five questions and the tool tells you whether to let it through or stop it. Everything runs on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Demo only. The tool learned from simulated transactions (PaySim), not real MTN or Orange customers. Amounts are shown in FCFA to make them easy to read. Built by Bih Brandy Sango.
